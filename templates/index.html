How It Works

Jump in, sketch the clues, and conquer the leaderboard in six straightforward steps

1
🎯

Choose a Mode

Pick either Guess the Song (English hits, Hollywood OSTs) or Guess the Movie (blockbusters & classics).

2
🚪

Create or Join

Create a private room with custom rounds & timer, or paste a 5-letter code to join your friends in the lobby.

3
🎨

One Player Draws

Each round, a designated drawer receives a top-secret title and brings it to life using responsive canvas tools.

4
💬

Everyone Guesses

All other players watch the canvas strokes stream in real time and fire their guesses into the rapid chat box.

5
⏱️

Fastest Score More

Time-decay scoring rewards lightning reflexes. Correct guessers earn top points, and clear drawers earn bonuses!

6
🏆

Highest Score Wins

Drawers rotate every round. After the final showdown, celebrate on the podium and challenge each other to rematch!
